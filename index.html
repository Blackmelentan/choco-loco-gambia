About Choco Loco

Choco Loco Patisserie | Café | Lounge in Pipeline, The Gambia, was founded by Mrs. Rima Bourgi with a singular mission: to offer completely in-house prepared, from-scratch luxury desserts that bring genuine European chocolaterie artistry to Kairaba Avenue.

From our legendary "Brownies to Die For" and golden bubble waffles to French Crêpes Suzette, silky Lotus Biscoff cheesecakes, and our renowned "Build Your Own" dessert bar, everything is prepared fresh using 100% pure Belgian Callebaut chocolate. Whether you are dropping in for a morning croissant and Spanish latte or gathering in the evening with family in our relaxing air-conditioned lounge, we welcome you with warm Gambian hospitality.
